# How to Activate Google Login

To make the Google Login button work, you need to link this website to a free Firebase project.

## 1. Create a Firebase Project
1. Go to [https://console.firebase.google.com/](https://console.firebase.google.com/) and log in with your Google account.
2. Click **"Add project"** (or "Create a project").
3. Name it "Reimagined Gift" (or anything you like).
4. Disable Google Analytics (optional, simpler without it).
5. Click **"Create Project"**.

## 2. Enable Google Authentication
1. In your new project dashboard, click **"Build"** -> **"Authentication"** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **"Get Started"**.
3. Select **"Google"** from the list of Sign-in providers.
4. Click **"Enable"**.
5. Select your support email from the dropdown.
6. Click **"Save"**.

## 3. Register Your App
1. Click the **Gear icon (Settings)** next to "Project Overview" in the top left.
2. Scroll down to the "Your apps" section.
3. Click the **Web icon** (</>).
4. Nickname the app (e.g., "Gift Site").
5. Click **"Register app"**.

## 4. Get Your Config Code
1. Firebase will show you a code block with `const firebaseConfig = { ... };`.
2. **Copy the values** inside that object (apiKey, authDomain, etc.).
3. Open `js/main.js` in your project.
4. Find the `FIREBASE_CONFIG` object at the top.
5. **Paste your values** there to replace the placeholders.

## 5. Important: Authorize Your Domains
By default, Google Login only works on `localhost`.
1. Go back to **Authentication** -> **Settings** -> **Authorized domains**.
2. Add your GitHub Pages domain (e.g., `yourusername.github.io`).
3. If testing locally on a specific IP, add that too.

---
**Done!** Your "Sign in with Google" button will now work.
